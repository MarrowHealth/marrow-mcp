Give me an honest read of how I am doing today.

Call the marrow `daily_brief` tool first. If anything in it is flagged, call `baselines` for that metric to see the trend. Then answer in a few short lines: what is normal for me today, what is above or below my normal, how food and water stand against my targets, and one thing worth doing with the rest of the day. Use the brief's own phrases (normal for you, above your normal) rather than population ranges. Do not diagnose or alarm. If a stream is stale or missing, say so plainly instead of guessing.
